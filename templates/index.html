Cybersecurity Defense Checklist

Essential safeguards against sophisticated smishing and social engineering attacks.

🔑

Never Share OTPs or PINs

Banks and legitimate services will NEVER request your OTP or UPI PIN for deposits or refunds.

🔗

Scrutinize Masked Links

Avoid shorteners like bit.ly, tinyurl, or suspicious domains imitating real bank URLs.

⚠️

Beware of Fake Urgency

"Account blocked", "Power disconnect tonight", or "Legal notice" are tactics to cause panic.

🎁

Disregard Unsolicited Prizes

If you didn't enter a contest, you didn't win. Never pay "handling or tax fees" to claim rewards.

🏦

Verify Via Official Numbers

Always verify account issues by calling the official phone number listed on your physical bank card.

🚫

Block & Report Fraud Senders

Submit unknown fraudulent senders to the national cybercrime portal and report them in-app.
